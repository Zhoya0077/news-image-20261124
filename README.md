A newspaper-style front page summing up the world's news of **Tuesday, 24 November 2026**, built for the [make.fun competition "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261124).

## What the image shows

- **Lead story — markets:** Nvidia roars back as Jensen Huang returns as chief executive; shares jump 31% in a day, the Nasdaq climbs 6.5%, and the rally spreads across Asia on Tuesday.
- **India–Nepal:** the new Saryu-Kamala Express rolls into Janakpur on its first run, after being flagged off by the two prime ministers.
- **Portugal:** Lisbon is set for a cost-of-living protest outside parliament as MPs take the final vote on the 2027 state budget, days after thousands marched over living costs and housing.
- **Indonesia:** the search goes on for about 30 people still missing after the factory fire in Banten; 24 confirmed dead.
- **South Korea:** Seoul names a new foreign minister and intelligence chief.
- **Also today:** a Russian strike hits a medical facility in Zaporizhzhia, killing three; Guru Nanak Jayanti is observed across India.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the `entry.png` held in this repository to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). It uses no network and writes the same bytes on every run.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
